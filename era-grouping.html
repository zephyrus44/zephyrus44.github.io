Matchup model
Each world's score is its top-100 power × (1 + age bonus). The youngest world in the era gets no bonus and the oldest gets the full amount. Worlds are ranked by score and split into groups of 4, strongest first. The defaults reproduce October with .
Ages. Each world's age is its in-game Day counter on the grouping date (start date + offset, 00:00 UTC). Day counts for 1001–1111 were read from the Migration screen on Nov 1, 2026. New worlds open every Friday at 00:00 UTC and are marked ~ until they exist.

Schedule. Rounds run 4 weeks on, 4 weeks off from Oct 3, 2026, so ◀ ▶ step 56 days. The October grouping is reproduced exactly with offset 0.

Power. Top-100 power comes from Deng's Den, refreshed daily on your site by the GitHub Action. The game only counts active players (its cutoff isn't published), and migration can move power right as a round starts, so treat predicted warzones as likely, not certain. When a round's actual warzones appear on Deng's Den, they're shown next to the prediction.
