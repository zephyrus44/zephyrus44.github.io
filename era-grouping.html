Era Grouping

Which era every world falls into for a Glowworm's Ruse round, based on world age on the grouping date.
EraWorld ageWorldsCount
How it works. Each world's age is its in-game day counter on the grouping date (start date + offset, 00:00 UTC). Ages come from the migration-screen readings of Jul 13, 2026; gaps between readings are interpolated, and worlds after 1095 assume a weekly Friday launch. Worlds marked ~ are estimates from uneven gaps, so check them in game if they sit near an era edge.

Schedule. Rounds run 4 weeks on, 4 weeks off starting Oct 3, 2026, so ◀ ▶ step 56 days. Calibration. The October grouping (Mythic 1–48, Dominance 49–60, Conquest 61–72, Clash 73–84, Frontier 85–96) is reproduced exactly with the Oct 3 start, so offset 0 is calibrated. If a future round comes out one world off at an edge, nudge the day offset until it matches the in-game list.
