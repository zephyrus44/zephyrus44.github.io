Matchup model
Each world's score is its top-100 power × (1 + age bonus). The youngest world in the era gets no bonus and the oldest gets the full amount. Worlds are ranked by score and split into groups of 4, strongest first. The defaults reproduce October with .
Ages. Each world's age is its in-game Day counter on the era cutoff date (the round's first day, 00:00 UTC). The day offset moves the whole round, including its era cutoff and Matching day. Day counts for 1001–1111 were read from the Migration screen on Nov 1, 2026. New worlds open every Friday at 00:00 UTC and are marked ~ until they exist.

Schedule. Rounds run 4 weeks on, 4 weeks off from Oct 3, 2026, so ◀ ▶ step 56 days. Each round: Announcement (5 days), Matching (1 day), Matching Reveal & Prelude (5 days), Land of the Abyss (14 days), Closing (3 days). Eras are set by world age at the start of the round; warzones are set from power on Matching day.

Power. Top-100 power comes from dengsden.com.
