Matchup model
Each world's score is its top-200 power (top 100 for the October round) × (1 + age bonus). The youngest world in the era gets no bonus and the oldest gets the full amount. Worlds are ranked by score and split into groups of 4, strongest first. The defaults reproduce October with .

Ages. Each world's age is its in-game Day counter on the era cutoff date (the round's first day, 00:00 UTC). The day offset moves the whole round, including its era cutoff and Matching day. Day counts for 1001–1111 were read from the Migration screen on Nov 1, 2026. New worlds open every Friday at 00:00 UTC and are marked ~ until they exist.

Schedule. Rounds run 4 weeks on, 4 weeks off from Oct 3, 2026, so ◀ ▶ step 56 days. Each round: Announcement (5 days), Matching (1 day), Matching Reveal & Prelude (5 days), Land of the Abyss (14 days), Closing (3 days). Eras are set by world age at the start of the round; warzones are set from power on Matching day.

Vanguard. Vanguard (Upper Ring) worlds take only a few migrants. The game picks them when each migration event opens: the 5 worlds furthest above their era's average power. ★ marks the actual picks for rounds that have had their migration. ☆ marks dengsden.com's projected picks for the upcoming migration's round and later ones, based on today's standings. Lists are refreshed daily.

Editor. The editor moves players or whole alliances between worlds and recalculates each world's top-200 power from its top 400 players, so the players who move up to replace anyone leaving are counted. A moved player only counts in their new world if they make its top 200. Players marked inactive stop counting toward their world's power, and the next players move up. Migration rules are shown as warnings, not enforced.

Power. Warzones are matched on each world's top-200 power; the October round used top 100. Top-100 totals come from dengsden.com, and top-200 totals are added up weekly from its player rankings, on Thursdays, the weekday Matching falls on. By default, inactive players are counted in each world's power here, because the game doesn't publish who is inactive. The Leave out inactive players option in Matchup model estimates it instead: a player whose power hasn't gone up in a chosen time (7 to 28 days), based on the weekly player lists. The totals for each choice are worked out ahead of time, so switching is instant. The game leaves inactive players out of the actual warzone matchups, so a world with many inactive players in its top 200 may be matched lower than predicted. If you know who's inactive, mark them in the editor.
